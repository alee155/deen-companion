Privacy Policy

Deen Companion  •  Effective date: November 3, 2026  •  Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Deen Companion ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application on iOS and Android. Deen Companion is developed by DevSouq Technologies (Muhammad Ali Tahir), based in Pakistan.

In short: you can use the Quran, prayer times, Qibla and other reference features without an account. If you create an account to join prayer groups, using your email, Google or Apple, we store your name, email address, gender, prayer logs, group memberships and the photos ("snaps") you choose to share, on our servers. We never see your Google or Apple password. We use your location, only when you allow it, to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. The App shows advertisements served through Google AdMob on iPhone and Android, and asks for your permission or consent where the law or Apple requires it. We do not sell your personal data. You can delete your account and its data at any time from inside the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Using the App without an account

You can browse the Quran, Hadith, Duas, the Names of Allah, Islamic names, the Hijri calendar, the Zakat calculator, Qibla direction and prayer times without creating an account. In this mode we do not ask for your name or email address. Your Favorites and Recent Activity are saved only on your own device.

Without an account we do not store personal data about you. However, requests for prayer times and city search are processed by our servers and by our providers (Section 3). Such a request contains the coordinates or the search text you entered, your IP address and a random device ID that the App creates when it is installed. The IP address and device ID are used briefly to protect the service from abuse, and not to identify you. Coordinates are passed to Aladhan to calculate prayer times, and city searches are passed to Open-Meteo.

1.2 Account information

You can create an account with your email address and a password, or by continuing with your Google or Apple account. When you create an account, we collect and store:

  • Name and email address — to create your account, show your name to the people in your groups, and, for email accounts, send you verification and password-reset codes.
  • Password — for email accounts only. You choose it; we store it only in protected form, never in plain text. Accounts created with Google or Apple have no password.
  • Gender (male or female) — chosen at sign-up. It is used to offer the Special days feature, which applies only to female accounts, and is not shown to other users.
  • Time zone — so your prayer days begin and end at the right local time.
  • Profile picture and group pictures — only if you choose to add them.

If you continue with Google or Apple

  • Google: Google confirms who you are and gives our servers a signed token. We read your name and email address from it. We do not receive your Google password, your contacts, your files or other Google data.
  • Apple: Apple confirms who you are and gives our servers a signed token and a one-time authorization code. We read your email address and, only the first time you sign in with this App, your name (Apple does not send it again). If you choose "Hide My Email", Apple gives us a private relay address that forwards to your real one, and we never see your real email. We exchange the code with Apple and keep the resulting refresh token only so that we can ask Apple to end the link with your Apple ID if you delete your account. We never store the code itself, and ending the link is done on a best-effort basis.
  • Same email, one account: if you already have an account with the same verified email address, signing in with Google or Apple is linked to that account instead of creating a second one. A hidden Apple relay address is a different email, so it creates a separate account.
  • Neither Google nor Apple tells us your gender. After you sign in for the first time, the App asks you to choose it once.
  • Google and Apple handle your sign-in under their own privacy policies. We receive only what is described above.

1.3 Prayer logs, groups, streaks and points

When you log a prayer, we store which prayer it was, the day and time, and the points and streaks that result. Groups you create or join, your membership and role in them, and the progress that group members see (which prayers you logged, your streak and points) are stored on our servers. Other members of a group you belong to can see this progress, your name and your profile picture.

Each group also has a leaderboard. It ranks the members of that group by the points they have earned since they joined, and shows each member's name, rank, points, number of prayers logged and current streak. The leaderboard is calculated from the prayer logs described above, is shown only to the members of that group, and needs no extra information from you. Any member can also open another member's details in the group, which show the same streak, points and today's prayers. When you leave a group or delete your account, you are removed from its leaderboard.

1.4 Snaps (photos)

  • When you log a prayer you may take a photo and send it to groups you choose. We store the photo and which groups it was sent to.
  • A snap is visible only to the members of the groups you sent it to. Each person can open it once, for the time you set (a few seconds), and it becomes unavailable to everyone when that prayer's time ends.
  • You can remove a logged prayer (Undo) while its time is open, which also deletes its photos.
  • The App asks for access to your camera only so you can take these photos, and only when you use the feature. Photos stored in your device's gallery are not accessed.
  • Photos are stored with our image-hosting provider (Section 3).

1.5 Special days (female accounts)

Special days is optional and only for female accounts. When you turn it on we store the start and end dates you enter for your period. This is sensitive health-related information. We use it only to log your prayers automatically while the feature is on, and it is not shown to other users.

You may also upload one photo for each prayer. We store these photos so that your prayer can be logged on your behalf during that time. These photos are not shown to other users as snaps by you directly; they are used only to keep your streak and your group progress, as the feature describes. The dates and photos stay until you remove them in the App or delete your account.

1.6 Location data

If you use Prayer Times or Qibla Direction, the App asks for access to your device's location so it can calculate accurate prayer times and the direction of the Qibla. When you have an account, the coordinates are sent to our servers, which store them, rounded to two decimal places (about 1 km), with your account so your prayer times and reminders stay correct. They stay until you clear your location or delete your account. When you use the App without an account, the coordinates are used to fetch prayer times and are not linked to an identity. We do not use your location for advertising. You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings, and you can still choose a city manually where supported.

To calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction, your coordinates are also passed to the third-party providers listed in Section 3. The Qibla feature sends your coordinates to a content provider (ummahapi.com), and prayer times come from our servers or, as a fallback, directly from a prayer-time provider (Aladhan).

1.6a Zakat calculator

If you use the Zakat calculator, the amounts you enter (for example savings, gold or silver) are sent to a content provider (ummahapi.com) so it can work out the result. We do not link these amounts to your account. Using the calculator is optional.

1.7 Reports and blocking

If you report a snap or a person, we store the report (who was reported, the reason you chose, any note you add and the content concerned, including a reference to the photo) so we can review it. Reports are also emailed to our support inbox. If you block someone, we record that so their snaps are hidden from you. A person you block, or report, is not told about it.

1.8 Notifications

If you turn on prayer reminders or daily content notifications, the App schedules them on your device. We do not need to know your notification settings to send them, and you can change them at any time in the App or in your device settings.

1.9 Content saved on your device

Favorites, Recent Activity, your last-read position, your settings and cached reference content (Quran, Hadith, Duas and similar) are saved on your device. Cached content is ordinary app content, not personal data about you.

1.10 Advertising data (Google AdMob)

Deen Companion is a free app supported in part by advertising, on both iPhone and Android. We use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display ads. To deliver, measure and prevent fraud on those ads, the SDK automatically collects and shares certain data with Google, including:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID on Android, the advertising identifier on iOS, where your device settings allow it) and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address (which can be used to derive an approximate location for ad delivery — separate from the precise location described in Section 1.6, which is not shared with the ad SDK);
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic and performance information.

Ads may be personalized (based on interests and activity associated with your device) or non-personalized (not using that information). Which you see depends on your choices:

  • iPhone: where Apple requires it, the App asks for your permission through Apple's "App Tracking Transparency" prompt before ads can use your device's advertising identifier. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, we show non-personalized ads and the identifier is not used for tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: the App shows a consent form (through Google's User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time through the App's privacy settings.
  • Android: you can limit ad personalization, and reset or delete your Advertising ID, in Settings > Google > Ads.
  • On either platform you can also use Google's Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com.

We do not knowingly show personalized ads to people under 13 or of unknown age. Google's use of this data is described in its policy, "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). The App works the same whatever you choose; ads are shown either way, but personalized ads need your permission or consent where it is required.

1.11 Device and diagnostic information

Apart from the advertising SDK, we do not use a separate third-party analytics service. Standard crash and diagnostic information may be available to us through Apple's App Store Connect and the Google Play Console, which follow Apple's and Google's own privacy policies.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and secure your account, and to send verification and password-reset codes;
  • To calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction for your location;
  • To run prayer groups: logging prayers, sharing snaps, streaks, points and the leaderboard;
  • To offer the Special days feature to female accounts;
  • To keep the community safe: reviewing reports, removing content that breaks our Terms, and acting on blocks;
  • To display, measure and prevent fraud on the advertisements that support the free App;
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and technical problems.

We do not use your information for any purpose beyond these.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to data brokers. Information you put in a group (your name, picture, prayer progress and the snaps you send) is shared with the members of that group. Apart from that, we share data only with the service providers in Section 3 so the App can work, with Google for advertising (Section 1.10), and if the law requires us to. We do not send your Google or Apple account details anywhere except to verify your sign-in and, for Apple, to ask Apple to end the link when you delete your account. Depending on where you live, sharing data with an ad network may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under laws such as the CCPA/CPRA; see Sections 1.10 and 9 for your options.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Account data is kept until you delete it or your account, except as listed below.
  • Snap photos expire when that prayer's time ends. They are then removed from our image-hosting provider, which can take up to about 5 minutes. Removal there is made on a best-effort basis.
  • Special days dates and photos stay until you remove them in the App or delete your account.
  • Verification and password-reset codes expire after a short time, and are stored only in hashed form.
  • Delete your account: go to Profile > Delete account in the App. This removes your profile, saved location and prayer settings, group memberships, prayer logs, snaps, photos, streaks, points, blocks and Special days data from our servers. It works the same for accounts created with email, Google or Apple.
  • If you signed in with Apple, deleting your account also asks Apple to end the link between the App and your Apple ID (on a best-effort basis). You can check this in Settings > [your name] > Sign-In & Security > Sign in with Apple on your iPhone.
  • If you signed in with Google, you can also remove the App's access in your Google Account under Security > Your connections to third-party apps & services.
  • Reports you made, or that were made about you, may be kept after deletion for safety and legal reasons, with the link to the deleted account removed. The details written in a report and the photo reference remain, and report emails already sent cannot be recalled.
  • Backups of deleted data are not kept longer than needed for normal operation.
  • On your device, Favorites, Recent Activity, settings and cached content stay until you remove them or uninstall the App.
  • Advertising data is held by Google under its own retention practices.

If you cannot use the App to delete your account, email us and we will delete it for you.

7. Children's Privacy

Deen Companion is a general-audience app for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The App does not check your age, so we rely on you to meet this requirement. We do not knowingly serve personalized ads or share the advertising identifier for users known to be under 13 or of unknown age. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us below and we will delete it.

8. Data Security

Data sent between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are stored in protected form, and we never receive your Google or Apple password. Snaps are delivered through short-lived links, and access to them is limited to the groups they were sent to. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your data.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR (EEA/UK) or CCPA/CPRA (California), including the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or opt out of certain processing (including advertising-related sharing), and to withdraw consent. You can delete your account in the App (Section 6) and control advertising, tracking and consent as described in Section 1.10. For any other request, email us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

10. International Users

The App can be used worldwide. Your information may be processed on servers and by providers (Section 3) in countries other than your own, subject to their safeguards.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App changes. We will change the "Last updated" date above, and if the change is significant we will tell you in the App. Continued use after a change means you accept the revised policy.
