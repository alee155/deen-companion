1. About the App

Deen Companion is a free application for Muslims. It provides the Quran with audio, Hadith, Duas, the Names of Allah, Islamic names, prayer times and reminders, Qibla direction, a Hijri calendar, a Zakat calculator and daily educational talks (Bayan). It also lets signed-in users create or join private groups, log their prayers, share photo snaps with their groups, and build streaks and points together. Many features work without an account; the group features require one, which you can create with your email, or with your Google or Apple account. The App is supported in part by third-party advertising on iPhone and Android (Section 11).
