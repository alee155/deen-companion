Need help with Deen Companion? You are in the right place. Read the answers below, and if you still need help, email us.

Account

How can I sign in?

With your email and password, or by tapping Continue with Google or Continue with Apple (Apple is on iPhone only). Use the same method you used when you signed up.

I did not get my verification code

This applies to accounts created with an email and password. Check your spam folder first, then tap Resend code on the verification screen. Make sure the email address you typed is correct. If it still does not arrive, email us from that address. Accounts created with Google or Apple do not need a code.

Continue with Google does nothing, or shows an error

Check your internet connection and that your phone has a Google account added. Close the App and try again. If the Google window closes by itself, update Google Play services (Android) or the App, and try again. If it keeps happening, email us with your phone model and the App version.

Continue with Apple does not work, or I used "Hide My Email"

Make sure you are signed in to an Apple ID in your iPhone's Settings. If you chose Hide My Email, Apple gives the App a private address that forwards to your real one, so it counts as a different email. Always sign in with Apple again to reach the same account, and avoid creating a second account with your real email.

I signed up with Google or Apple. Can I also use a password?

Yes. On the sign-in screen tap Forgot Password?, enter your account's email, then enter the code we send and choose a password. You can then sign in either way. If you used Apple's Hide My Email, the code goes to the private address, which forwards to your real inbox.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot Password?, enter your email, then enter the code we send and choose a new password.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile > Delete account and confirm. This works for every sign-in method. Your profile, group memberships, prayer logs, snaps, photos, streaks and points are removed from our servers. If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to end the link between the App and your Apple ID, on a best-effort basis (you can check it in iPhone Settings > your name > Sign-In & Security > Sign in with Apple). If you cannot get into the App, email us from your account's email address and we will delete it for you. See our account deletion page and our Privacy Policy for details.

I picked the wrong gender, or I was asked for it after signing in

Google and Apple do not share gender, so the App asks for it once after your first sign-in with either. It can be set only once, because it controls the Special days feature. If it was chosen by mistake, email us.

Prayer times and reminders

My prayer times look wrong

Allow location access, then open the prayer times calculation settings and choose the calculation method used by your local mosque. Times are estimates, so please confirm them with your local authority when it matters.

Reminders do not ring on time

Make sure notifications are allowed for Deen Companion, and on Android allow alarms and turn off battery optimisation for the App. On iPhone check that Focus or silent settings are not blocking notifications.

Groups and snaps

How does a snap work?

When a prayer's time is open, tap the camera, take a photo, and choose the groups to send it to. Each person can open your snap once, and it expires when the prayer's time ends. You can send another photo to groups that have not received one yet. Undo removes your prayer and every photo you sent for it while its time is open.

The camera button is grey

It is grey when no prayer time is open, when every group already has your snap, when none of your groups has another member yet, while Special days are on, or when prayer times have not loaded yet (check your internet connection and location). Tap it and the App tells you which one applies.

How does the leaderboard work?

Open a group and tap Leaderboard next to View statistics. Members are ranked 1, 2, 3 and so on by the points they have earned since they joined that group, so someone who joined later is not behind because of the days before they were there. The top three are shown on a podium, and everyone else is listed below with prayers logged and streak. Your own row is highlighted. Pull down to refresh. Only members of the group can see it.

The leaderboard looks wrong or does not load

Check your internet connection and tap Try again. Points come from the prayers logged in that group, and a prayer removed with Undo also removes its points. If a number still looks wrong, email us with the group name and what you expected.

How do I join or invite someone to a group?

Open the group and share its invitation link. Anyone with the link can ask to join. Group administrators can reset the link or remove members.

How do I report or block someone?

Open a snap and tap the ⋯ menu, or tap the ⋯ next to a member's name, then choose Report or Block. Blocked people are listed in Settings > Blocked users, where you can unblock them. You can also email us with the details and we will look into it. We review reports by hand and may remove content or accounts that break our Terms.

What does blocking someone do?

Blocking only hides that person's snaps from you, and yours from them. Everything else stays the same: points, the leaderboard, streaks and group membership do not change, and the blocked person can still send snaps to a group you share. If you do not want to be in a group with someone, you can leave the group. Blocked people are not told.

Ads

Why do I see ads?

Deen Companion is free, and ads help us keep it that way. They come from Google AdMob. They never change what you can do in the App.

How do I control personalized ads?

On iPhone, the App asks for your permission before ads can use your advertising identifier. You can change that in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, use Settings > Google > Ads. In Europe, the UK and Switzerland, the App shows a consent form first. You can also use Google's Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not personalized.

An ad is inappropriate

Please email us with a screenshot and your phone model, and we will report it to the ad network.
