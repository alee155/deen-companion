Delete Your Deen Companion Account

Deen Companion  •  Developed by DevSouq Technologies

This page explains how to delete your Deen Companion account and the data linked to it. It applies to accounts created with email, Google or Apple, on iPhone and Android.

Delete your account in the App (fastest)

  1. Open Deen Companion and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Can't open the App? Request deletion by email

Email devsouqtechnologies@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject Delete my Deen Companion account. If you signed in with Apple using Hide My Email, write from the email you normally use and tell us the name on the account, so we can match it. We reply as soon as we can, usually within a few working days, and delete the account after we have confirmed it is yours. Never send your password.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address, gender and profile picture;
  • Your saved location and prayer settings;
  • Your group memberships (a group you were the last member of is deleted, otherwise admin rights pass to another member);
  • Your prayer logs, streaks and points;
  • Every snap you sent;
  • Your blocks;
  • Your Special days data: the dates and the photos;
  • Pending verification codes.

Your snaps, profile picture and Special days photos are also removed from our image-hosting provider. This is done on a best-effort basis, so we cannot guarantee that every copy is removed at the same moment.

If you signed in with Apple, deleting your account also ends the link between the App and your Apple ID (we ask Apple to revoke the App's access, on a best-effort basis). If you signed in with Google, you can also remove the App's access in your Google Account under Security > Your connections to third-party apps & services.

What may be kept

  • Reports about content or people, and reports you made, may be kept for safety and legal reasons, with the link to your deleted account removed. The details written in a report and the photo reference remain.
  • Report emails that were already sent to our support inbox cannot be recalled.
  • On your phone, Favorites, settings and cached content stay until you uninstall the App.
  • Advertising data held by Google under its own policies is not stored by us.
Deleting your account cannot be undone. If you only want to stop using the App, you can sign out or uninstall it instead. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle your data.
